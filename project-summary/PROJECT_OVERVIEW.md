# NewsAI

NewsAI la mot nen tang tong hop, xu ly va quan tri tin tuc duoc thiet ke theo huong san pham hoa, khong chi dung lai o muc crawl du lieu. Muc tieu cua he thong la bien mot quy trinh von phan tan va ton nhieu thao tac thu cong thanh mot pipeline van hanh ro rang: lay bai viet tu nguon tin, trich xuat noi dung, tom tat bang AI, tao tai nguyen bo sung nhu audio, sau do dua toan bo ket qua ve mot giao dien quan tri de theo doi va dieu phoi.

Ve kien truc, du an duoc tach thanh cac thanh phan ro rang de de phat trien va mo rong ve sau. Frontend su dung Next.js de xay dung giao dien nguoi dung va khu vuc admin. Backend su dung FastAPI, SQLAlchemy va PostgreSQL de cung cap API, quan ly du lieu va xu ly nghiep vu. Cac tac vu nen nhu crawl, tom tat va xu ly noi dung duoc dua qua Celery va Redis, giup he thong khong bi khoa boi cac cong viec ton thoi gian. Luong AI hien tai duoc cau hinh qua OpenAI API voi model `gpt-4o-mini`, trong khi phan chuyen van ban thanh giong noi duoc tach rieng qua dich vu TTS.

Diem manh lon nhat cua NewsAI nam o tinh thuc dung trong van hanh. He thong cho phep cau hinh nguon tin, chu de va lich chay theo nhu cau thuc te thay vi hard-code quy trinh. Cach to chuc nay giup no phu hop voi ca nhu cau noi bo lan dinh huong phat trien thanh mot san pham SaaS nho. Ben canh do, viec tach rieng `models`, `schemas`, `services` va `api` giup codebase giu duoc do ro rang can thiet khi du an bat dau lon dan. Day khong phai la mot codebase chi de demo tinh nang AI, ma la mot nen tang co chu y den kha nang bao tri, thay doi cau hinh, theo doi tien trinh xu ly va mo rong them workflow moi.

Neu nhin du an o goc do ky thuat va san pham, NewsAI co gia tri o cho no ket hop duoc ba lop thuong bi tach roi trong nhieu he thong nho: tu dong hoa noi dung, quan tri van hanh va kha nang mo rong. Voi nen mong hien tai, du an co the tiep tuc phat trien theo nhieu huong nhu nang cap chat luong tom tat, bo sung danh gia nguon tin, them co che kiem duyet noi dung, hoac mo rong thanh he thong tong hop tin tuc theo linh vuc chuyen sau.
